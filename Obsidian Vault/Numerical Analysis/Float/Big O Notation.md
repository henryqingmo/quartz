Big O means there exists a constant $C$ such that after some index $n_0$, The sequence is bounded by constant multiple of the big O sequence.

Small O looks at the asymptotic behaviour, and say the sequence is negligible compared to the small O sequence.

[[chap2_Float.pdf#page=19&rect=73,198,430,407|chap2_Float, p.19]]
When we uses approach instead of an index $n_0$, we allows for infinity.
