### Idea
#### Multi-Variable
![[Pasted image 20250622161403.png]]
We require for all $x$, $P_{X|Y} = P_{X}$, this means that the condition will not result in the change of the probability $P_{X}$ for any $x$.
#### Example
![[Pasted image 20250622161447.png]]
$P_{X,Y}(x,y) = P_{X}(x)P_{Y}(y)$, We basically reset the sample space to be the condition, so divide each probability by the probability of the condition $\frac{9}{20}$.
