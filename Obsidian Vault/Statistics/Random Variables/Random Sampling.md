### Idea
![[Pasted image 20250708150113.png]]
> [!note]
>We can collect the outcome of $n$ independent  repetitions of the same experiment, and collect the observed value into a vector. 

![[Pasted image 20250708150224.png]]
> [!abstract]
>We can now define a sample space, being every possible sequence of outcomes. Since these outcomes are [[Independent Event]], the probability for obtaining one such outcome can be expressed as the products.  
>We then define a [[Statistics/Random variable|Random variable]] that maps these outcome to some value.
![[Pasted image 20250708150639.png]]
This is known as an **i.i.d** sample.
#### Sample Mean
![[Pasted image 20250708150747.png]]
> [!note]
>We define a new [[Statistics/Random variable|Random variable]], as the average outcomes of the sample. 

![[Pasted image 20250708150857.png]]
Now we can calculate the [[Mean]] and [[Variance and Standard Deviation]] of the random variable of sample mean.
> [!note]
> We can Observe that as n increases, the variance is smaller, indicating the average gets closer to the expected value.
