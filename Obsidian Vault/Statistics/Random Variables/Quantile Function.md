> [!note]
> Here the $inf$ represents **infimum**, which is the [[Hasse diagram|Greatest Lower Bound]] of the set. 
>>[!abstract] 
>> Look at all $x$ such that the [[Cumulative Distribution Function]] is at least $\alpha$.
>> Among all those $x$, pick the smallest one, (the left most point). 
>This is to ensure function that's not strictly increasing (having flat regions), therefore doesn't have an inverse, the $inf$ ensures we pick the left-most point.
