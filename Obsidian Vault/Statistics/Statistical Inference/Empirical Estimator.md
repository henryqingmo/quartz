### Idea
![[Pasted image 20250726132320.png]]
> [!note]
> Here we are trying to estimate the fix probability $P(X \leq y)$
> Extreme case, only 1 sample, we will have the sample space of all population, and the estimator maps all of the population $\leq y$ to 1, other to 0. Which makes it a [[Bernoulli Distribution]].
> Extreme case, taking $n \rightarrow \infty$, and 1 sample, we will have get exactly the average of all the population that's less than $y$, and divide that by the entire population, which gives us exactly the probability.
>
