### Idea
![[Pasted image 20250805180100.png|500]]
The expansion of $x^TAx$, if positive definite, it will be the sum of two squares, if positive semi definite, it will be one square, if not it will be the difference between two squares.


![[Pasted image 20250805180410.png]]
For non-positive definite matrix, the cross-section will be a hyperbole

![[Pasted image 20250805180458.png]]
For positive definite matrix, the cross-section will be an ellipse

![[Pasted image 20250805182923.png]]
We can find the pivots, as their product equals the determinant. 

#### Property
![[Pasted image 20250901162043.png]]
Every positive definite matrix is [[Self-Adjoint and Hermitian]] and therefore [[Normal Maps]].
